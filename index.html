Approach

Engineering, not just installation.

Most production lines run well below what their equipment is capable of. The constraint is rarely the machinery — it's control logic written years ago, instrumentation never properly commissioned, and data that exists but is never used.

We combine automation programming with a licensed electrical contracting capability, so the same specialist designs the change, implements it and certifies it. Nothing is lost in handover between trades, and accountability sits in one place.

Work is documented to a standard your team can maintain — readable code, labelling that matches the drawings, and records that stay accurate after we leave.

New builds & innovation

From concept to commissioned.

New lines and greenfield installations start from a blank sheet, which is where the largest gains are available — and where decisions made early determine what the plant can do for the next fifteen years.

We take a concept through functional design, control philosophy, build, commissioning and handover. Where a client has an idea that doesn't yet exist as a product, we scope what it would take, give an honest assessment of whether it stacks up, and build it if it does.

If it can be specified, it can be engineered.

Sector

Food and beverage production.

Our experience is in high-speed FMCG manufacturing, and that focus is deliberate. The constraints in food and beverage are specific: hygiene and washdown requirements, allergen and changeover control, traceability obligations, and a cost of downtime measured in pallets per hour.

Designing within those constraints from the outset produces a different result to adapting a general-purpose solution afterwards.

We work with producers across the Gold Coast, Brisbane and South East Queensland.
