Where we work

Food and beverage is what we know

Most of our years have been spent on high-speed FMCG lines — beverage filling, bakery, dairy. Blow moulders, fillers, labellers, palletisers, conveyor systems and the controls that tie them together.

That background means less time spent explaining your plant to us and more time fixing what's actually wrong. We've stood in front of a stopped line at 4am with a shift supervisor waiting, and we understand what every minute costs.

How we work

We listen first, then build it properly

Good automation work starts with understanding what the people running the line actually need — not what looks tidy on a drawing. So we ask, we watch the process, and we take the operators seriously.

Then we build it the way we'd want to find it ourselves: neat terminations, code that the next person can read, labelling that matches the drawings, and documentation that's still true in two years. That's the craft of it, and it's the part most people cut corners on.

The aim is simple — you should end up with something that works better than you asked for.
